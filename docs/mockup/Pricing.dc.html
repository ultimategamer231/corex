Pricing questions

What happens if we do not renew the managed plan?

After 30 days without payment, we export all your data, send it to you, and pause your system. Nothing is deleted without warning.

Can we switch from Managed to Handover later?

Yes. We quote the difference and hand over the code and your data.

Can we resell the software after a Handover?

No. The license covers use and changes within your own organization only.
