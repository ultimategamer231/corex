Software built in Liberia, for how organizations here work

Corex Digital Solutions is a software studio based in Monrovia. We build systems for schools, clinics, businesses and NGOs, and we let every client test a working demo before they pay.

We design for the realities our clients deal with: unreliable internet, payments in both US and Liberian dollars, and staff who need training as much as they need software.

What we believe

Test before you pay

You should see software working with realistic data before you commit money to it.

Your data is yours

Every client gets a separate system, and can take all of its data with it at any time.

Honest scope

We say clearly what a system does and does not do, and we put it in writing before we start.
